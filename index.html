Homelings
Ages 3 to 6, and sums for 6 to 8

Help the animals get home

A calm puzzle game for little ones. Place road tiles so the bus, the boat or the tractor can take each animal home. No reading needed, no timers, and no way to lose.

iPhone and iPad version planned.

How it plays

Each level is a small board. Children drag or tap road pieces into place, then press Go and watch the ride.

1. Build the road

Tile by tile, from each animal to its home. Drops are forgiving, and a hint glows if a child gets stuck.

2. Press Go

The vehicle drives the road you built. If the road stops short, the animal just shrugs: try again, no penalty.

3. Make friends

Every ride home earns a sticker for the animal album and parts for the garage.

An island to explore

Chapters open one after another across the island.

Boats, tractors and diggers

Each vehicle has its own roads: rivers for the boat, fields for the tractor.

Sums become roads

For ages 6 to 8: gates add and take away apples on the way home. Parents choose whether it is on.

Our promise to families

No ads, ever

Not in the free game, not in the full game. Nothing to skip, and nothing to tap by mistake.

No server, no personal data

Made for children

No chat and no links where children play. Purchases, links and settings sit behind a grown-up check.
